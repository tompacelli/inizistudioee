Who we are

This website is run by Inizi OÜ, a film production company based in Tallinn, Estonia (“Inizi”, “we”). We are the controller of the personal data described below. You can reach us at hello [at] inizi.studio.

What we collect, and why

  • Messages you send us. If you use the contact form we receive what you type into it (name, email, optional company, interest and timing, and your message); if you email us we receive your address, your message and anything you attach. The form is delivered to our inbox by Web3Forms, which processes it on our behalf and does not use it for its own purposes. We use it to reply and to run the project you ask about (legal basis: your request, or our legitimate interest in answering enquiries). We keep it for as long as the conversation or project needs, then as long as the law requires.
  • Server logs. Our hosting provider records technical data such as IP address, time and requested page, to deliver and secure the site (legitimate interest). These logs are kept for a short period.
  • Analytics, only if you accept. If you press Accept in the cookie banner we load Google Analytics 4, which sets cookies and reports which pages are visited, roughly where from and on what kind of device. We have switched off advertising features and Google signals. Legal basis: your consent. If you decline, or your browser sends a Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal, nothing is loaded from Google.

We do not run advertising, we do not sell personal data and the only form on this site is the contact form, which sends nothing until you press Send.

Who receives data

Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC receive analytics data, only if you accept. Our hosting and email providers process data on our behalf. When you follow a link to Instagram (Meta) or Canva you leave this site and their own privacy policies apply; we embed nothing from them.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask us for access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction or a portable copy, and you may object to processing based on legitimate interest or withdraw consent at any time. Write to hello [at] inizi.studio. You may also complain to the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate (Andmekaitse Inspektsioon, aki.ee) or to your local supervisory authority.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS, loads no third-party fonts or scripts except analytics you accept, and its only form (contact) is sent over HTTPS with a spam trap and no tracking. Contact addresses are not written into the page source, to reduce spam.
