Two filmmakers, one mission.

Inizi was founded by Tom Pacelli and Johanna-Liisa Meema, brought together by a shared love for storytelling and the belief that every brand has a story worth telling. Our passion lies in capturing the beauty, emotion, and details that make an experience truly memorable.

Our journey has taken us across the world, capturing moments that reflect the elegance and authenticity of some of the world’s most remarkable destinations.

02The team

Johanna-Liisa Meema

Co-founder & Creative Director

Johanna’s background in marketing adds a deeper dimension to her emotion-driven storytelling, where the focus is not only on visuals, but also on authenticity, brand alignment, and meaningful connection. Her approach combines strategic thinking with high-quality visuals to create content that genuinely resonates with audiences.

Tom Pacelli

Co-founder & Director / DP

Tom is a multiple award-winning creative with extensive international experience. He combines technical mastery with an instinctive sense for atmosphere and emotion. At the core of his work is authenticity and the moments that unfold naturally, transforming into compelling visuals that leave a lasting impression.

Supported by a world-class team of creatives, we craft visuals that feel natural while remaining intentionally refined.

Our mission is to create campaigns that reflect the essence of your brand through elegant and immersive visual experiences.
